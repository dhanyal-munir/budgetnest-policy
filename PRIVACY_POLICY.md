# BudgetNest Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

**Developer:** Muhammad Danyal Munir

**Contact:** danyalmunir.dev@gmail.com

BudgetNest is a local-first budgeting app. This policy explains what stays on your phone and what, if anything, leaves it.

## 1. Data Stored on Your Device

Your expenses, income, categories, budgets, subscriptions, goals, wallets, notifications, settings, business records, receipt photos and profile photo are stored on your phone. BudgetNest works fully without an account.

## 2. Optional Cloud Backup (Premium)

Cloud backup is an optional Premium feature and may become available in a current or future app update. If you choose to create an account and back up, we store the following in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore):

- Your email address and a password managed by Firebase Authentication.
- A copy of your expenses, categories, budgets, subscriptions, goals, wallets, notifications, settings and business records.

Receipt photos, your profile photo and business logos are never uploaded. Only your signed-in account can read or write your backup. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google Firebase.

If you do not create an account, no data leaves your phone except as described in the Advertising and Purchases sections below.

## 3. Delete Your Account and Data

If you have created a cloud backup account, you can delete it in either of these ways:

- **In the app:** go to Settings → Cloud backup → Delete account. This deletes your account and cloud backup.
- **Without the app:** email danyalmunir.dev@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and backup within 30 days.

**Data retention:** Your backup is deleted from our database when you delete your account. Google Firebase may keep copies in its automated backups for a short period before they are permanently removed. We do not keep any other copy of your data.

Deleting your account does not remove data stored on your phone. Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove it.

## 4. Advertising

Free users see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can limit ad personalization in your device's Google settings. Premium and the one-time Remove Ads purchase hide ads.

## 5. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details.

## 6. Receipt Scanning (Premium)

When available, receipt text is read on your device with Google ML Kit. The photo is not sent to our servers.

## 7. Permissions

- **Camera and photos:** to attach receipts.
- **Notifications:** for reminders.
- **Internet:** for ads, purchases and optional backup.

Core features work without camera or photo access.

## 8. Exports and Support

Exports (CSV, PDF, Excel) are created on your phone and shared only through the share sheet you choose.

## 9. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

## 10. Children

BudgetNest is not directed to children under 13.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
